Workshop Overview

Deploying soft robotic technologies in challenging natural environments is a critical step toward realizing their full potential. Beyond drawing inspiration from nature, Soft Robotics in the Wild extends to one of humanity’s most fundamental needs: food production and agriculture. Crops are delicate and variable, fields are unstructured, and labour is scarce. Yet soft robots that work in the wild will not be hand-tuned; they will be designed, trained, and adapted by AI. Soft robotics offers physical compliance, conformability, and damage-free interaction with produce, but conventional design and control pipelines struggle with the nonlinear mechanics, high-dimensional design spaces, and complex contact dynamics of soft contact in the food industry. Modern AI – spanning neural surrogate physics modelling, multi-objective evolutionary algorithms, reinforcement learning, and data-driven visual-tactile perception – offers unprecedented opportunities to automate soft robotic synthesis and enable real-time control.

This half-day workshop explores the synergy between AI methodologies and soft mechatronics for field agriculture across three interconnected domains: Computational Design for Agriculture, Intelligent Perception and Tactile Sensing in Agricultural Deployment, and Learning-Based Control for Real-World Agricultural Deployment. Together, these domains aim to translate laboratory advances into harvesting, crop handling, post-harvest operations, and food waste processing. The programme features keynote talks from leading experts, technical presentations, and a highly interactive demo session, creating a vibrant environment for intellectual exchange and collaboration.
