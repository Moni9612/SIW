RoboSoft 2027 • 5–9 April 2027 • Brisbane, Australia

Types

This workshop invites contributions in the form of extended abstracts and poster submissions. We welcome late-breaking results or recently published work that you wish to share in a collaborative setting. Extended abstracts should be concise, with a maximum length of 1 page (using the IEEE conference format), including all text, figures, and references. Please submit your work in PDF format for consideration. Your insights and findings will enrich the workshop by fostering a dynamic exchange of ideas among participants.

Topics

We welcome contributions addressing, but not limited to, the following topics:

  • AI-Driven Soft Robotic Design & Optimization
  • Intelligent soft materials and actuation
  • Learning-based control of soft robots
  • Sim-to-real transfer for compliant systems
  • Soft robotic grasping for Agricultural robotics
  • Visual-Tactile Sensing & Perception in Clutter
  • Field deployment of soft robots in unstructured environments
  • Differentiable simulation and physics-informed learning for soft bodies
  • Benchmarking and datasets for soft robotics in agriculture
